How do I book an appointment?

Send a request through the form above, call +352 26 55 07 66, email info@customink.lu, or DM on Instagram. Every request is confirmed personally ΓÇö an appointment is only fixed once the studio confirms it with you.

Do I need a consultation first?

Yes ΓÇö it is part of how the studio works. Larger projects (sleeves, back pieces, cover-ups) always start with an in-studio consultation, where the right artist is matched to your idea.

How much does a tattoo cost?

It depends on size, style, placement and sessions needed ΓÇö so quotes are given personally, after a consultation and before anything is booked. You will always know the expected cost before the needle starts.

Can I bring references or just an idea?

Both are welcome. A photo, a sketch or an idea in words ΓÇö the artists draw every project individually and refine it with you until it feels right.

Yes ΓÇö cover-ups and reworkings are a dedicated service. Bring the tattoo you want to transform to a consultation and the artist will talk you through what is possible.

Ear lobes, helix and cartilage, nose and septum, lip and medusa, tongue, navel and more. The piercer advises you on placement and jewellery first, and answers every question before anything happens.

How should I prepare for my session?

Sleep well, eat properly beforehand, and avoid alcohol for 24 hours. Wear comfortable clothing with easy access to the area, and mention any health conditions or medication when booking.

What does aftercare involve?

You receive personal aftercare instructions before leaving, and the studio stays reachable while you heal. In short: keep it clean, follow the instructions you were given, and reach out if anything concerns you.

How long does healing take?

Tattoos usually surface-heal in two to four weeks, with full healing taking longer. Piercings vary a lot by placement. Your artist or piercer gives you a realistic timeline for your piece.
