1. What this website does not do

This website sets no cookies, stores no personal data on any server, and uses no tracking or advertising tools by default.

2. Appointment requests

The appointment request form does not send data to a server. When you submit it, your own email application opens with a pre-filled draft addressed to info@customink.lu. Nothing is transmitted until you press “send” in your email application. Your request is then processed by the studio solely to answer your enquiry and organise your appointment.

3. Third-party content

The map in the “Visit the studio” section is embedded from Google Maps. When the section loads, Google receives technical data such as your IP address. The same applies when you open external links to Instagram, Facebook or Google Maps. These services are operated under the responsibility of their respective providers (Google Ireland Ltd., Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.).

4. Server logs

The hosting provider of this website processes technical access data (e.g. IP address, time of access) in server logs, as is standard for website delivery and security. [Hosting provider and retention period: to be completed by the studio.]
