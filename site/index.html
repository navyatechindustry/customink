01 — The studio

A studio built around
the person wearing it.

Custom Ink is a tattoo and piercing atelier in Remerschen, on the Moselle side of Luxembourg. Resident artists and international guests work under one roof, in every style — from a first fine-line piece to a full back project.

Nothing here is templated. Your idea is talked through, drawn and refined until it belongs to you — then executed with the patience a lifetime piece deserves. That is the whole philosophy: your skin, your story.

06 — The experience

From idea to ink.

Five steps, no surprises — here’s how a piece travels from a sentence to healed skin.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    You bring an idea — a photo, a sketch, a sentence. We talk style, size, placement and budget, openly. You leave knowing exactly what is possible and what it takes.

  2. 02

    Design

    The matched artist draws your piece individually. Nothing is inked until the design is right in your eyes — revisions are part of the process, not an extra.

  3. 03

    Preparation

    Fresh, sterile single-use setup; the station prepared for your project; the stencil positioned and checked together before anything begins.

  4. 04

    The session

    Calm studio, good music, breaks whenever you need them. The artists work precisely and check in with you throughout — clients keep mentioning how at ease they felt.

  5. 05

    Aftercare

    You leave with personal healing instructions and the studio stays reachable while you heal. Questions at day three? Just message — that is what the team is for.

07 — Hygiene & safety

Clean is the baseline.
We treat it that way.

Nervous about the needle part? That is normal — and it is exactly where this studio refuses to improvise. Every session works with sterile, single-use needles and cartridges; stations are disinfected and re-prepared for every client; gloves and barrier protection are standard, not exceptions.

Ask us anything about materials and process before we start — the team would rather answer ten questions than have you hesitate through one.

11 — FAQ

Asked before
every first needle.

How do I book an appointment?

Send a request through the form above, call +352 26 55 07 66, email info@customink.lu, or DM on Instagram. Every request is confirmed personally — an appointment is only fixed once the studio confirms it with you.

Do I need a consultation first?

Yes — it is part of how the studio works. Larger projects (sleeves, back pieces, cover-ups) always start with an in-studio consultation, where the right artist is matched to your idea.

How much does a tattoo cost?

It depends on size, style, placement and sessions needed — so quotes are given personally, after a consultation and before anything is booked. You will always know the expected cost before the needle starts.

Can I bring references or just an idea?

Both are welcome. A photo, a sketch or an idea in words — the artists draw every project individually and refine it with you until it feels right.

Do you do cover-ups or rework old tattoos?

Yes — cover-ups and reworkings are a dedicated service. Bring the tattoo you want to transform to a consultation and the artist will talk you through what is possible.

What piercings do you offer?

Ear lobes, helix and cartilage, nose and septum, lip and medusa, tongue, navel and more. The piercer advises you on placement and jewellery first, and answers every question before anything happens.

How should I prepare for my session?

Sleep well, eat properly beforehand, and avoid alcohol for 24 hours. Wear comfortable clothing with easy access to the area, and mention any health conditions or medication when booking.

What does aftercare involve?

You receive personal aftercare instructions before leaving, and the studio stays reachable while you heal. In short: keep it clean, follow the instructions you were given, and reach out if anything concerns you.

How long does healing take?

Tattoos usually surface-heal in two to four weeks, with full healing taking longer. Piercings vary a lot by placement. Your artist or piercer gives you a realistic timeline for your piece.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Yes — life happens. Contact the studio as early as you can and a new time will be found.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Most projects are planned in advance so the right artist and enough time are reserved for you. For smaller pieces, send a message — the studio will tell you the earliest availability, including any same-day openings.

How old do I have to be?

Tattoos and piercings follow Luxembourg’s legal requirements. Bring a valid ID, and contact the studio about minimum ages and parental consent before booking.
